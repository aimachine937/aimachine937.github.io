AI agent setup

An AI agent solves a defined task in your systems: it reads, finds information and prepares a result that a person approves. We build it, test it with you and hand it over up and running.

What an agent typically does

  • Customer service: reads incoming email, finds the order and drafts a reply
  • Internal knowledge: answers staff from your handbooks, procedures and contracts
  • Quotes: drafts a quote from the enquiry, price list and past cases
  • Invoices and receipts: extracts data and routes exceptions to a person
  • Meetings: writes notes, task lists and follow-up emails after Teams meetings

How we build it

  1. Week 1

    Scope

    One task, one goal and one owner on your side. We measure the baseline.

  2. Weeks 2 to 3

    Build

    The agent gets access to exactly the data it needs and nothing else.

  3. Weeks 3 to 4

    Test and go live

    Trial with real cases, adjustment and short documentation.

Choosing a platform

If you work in Microsoft 365, we usually build in Copilot Studio so the agent respects your existing permissions. If you are small or use Google Workspace, ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini is often cheaper. If the agent must work across many systems, we use n8n or Make. The choice of platform follows your systems.

Questions

What is the difference between a chatbot and an AI agent?

A chatbot answers questions. An agent can also act: look things up in your systems, fill in fields and prepare a case that a person then approves.

Can the agent send things by itself?

Only if you decide so. By default the agent prepares, and a person approves anything sent to customers or posted to your accounts.

Do customers need to know they are talking to AI?

Yes. Since August 2026 the EU AI Act requires transparency, so people can tell when they are dealing with an AI system. We always set up customer-facing agents so this is clear.

What does the agent cost to run?

On top of operation and maintenance, you pay the provider's usage directly. For most SMEs it is a small monthly amount. We estimate it before we build.
