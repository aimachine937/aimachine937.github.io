AI workshop for leadership

Half a day where owners and leadership decide what AI should be used for in your business. Not a tools course, but a decision meeting with a clear plan as the outcome.

What we cover

  • Where time really goes in your business and where AI can take part of it
  • Realistic expectations: what AI does well today and what it does not
  • Data, security and accountability: what leadership must decide
  • Choosing the first two tasks, with owner, goal and timeline
  • Budget: licences, setup and the option of an SMV:Digital grant

What you get afterwards

  • A written decision note with prioritised tasks
  • An estimate of price and time for the first setups
  • A tool recommendation for your systems

Who it is for

Owner managers, executives and leadership teams in small and medium-sized businesses, typically 3 to 10 participants. On site or online. It is about direction and decisions for the business.

Questions

Is it a course?

No. It is a working session for leadership where you make decisions about your own business. The result is a written plan with owner, goal and budget for the first tasks.

Do we need to prepare?

A short questionnaire before the workshop and ideally a list of the tasks that take the most time today.

Can the workshop be part of an SMV:Digital programme?

Yes, analysis and planning can be part of a grant programme if it is in your project description.
