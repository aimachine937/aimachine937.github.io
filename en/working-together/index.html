How we work

You should know what you are paying for before we start and what you have when we finish. Here is how working together goes and what is delivered.

What you receive

After a clarity session or workshop

Decision note

Two to four pages with prioritised tasks, recommended tool, estimated price and time, and the first concrete steps.

After mapping

Process overview and business case

The workflows reviewed, where the time goes and what is worth automating, in order.

After setup

Operating documentation

What the solution does, which data it uses, who owns it, how to switch it off and what to do if it fails.

After secure setup

AI policy and risk overview

A short policy for staff and an overview of access and sharing issues found and fixed.

After a grant programme

Implementation plan

Analysis, recommendations and a plan that can be used in the report to SMV:Digital.

Always

Access to everything

All accounts, setups and documents stay with you. Nothing is locked to us.

Fixed price or hourly rate

Almost everything we do has a fixed price. That moves the risk from you to us: if a job takes longer than expected, that is our problem. In return, the scope is agreed in writing before we start, so both sides know what is included.

We only use hourly rates for small adjustments and for SMV:Digital programmes, where the rules require invoices to show hours and hourly rate.

  • Fixed price: clarity session, mapping, setups, workshop and secure setup
  • Monthly price: ongoing advisory and agent operation
  • Hourly rate: ad hoc tasks and grant programmes

  • We do not resell licences or take commission from vendors

  • We do not run courses. Handover is part of the setup

Questions

What do licences and usage cost on top?

It depends on the tool and how much the solution is used. We estimate it before we build, and you pay the provider directly on your own account.

What if the job turns out bigger than agreed?

Then we stop and agree it with you before more hours are spent. You never get an invoice you have not agreed to.

Can a solution be changed afterwards?

Yes. Smaller changes are done at the hourly rate or as part of operation and maintenance.

Do you work alone?

Yes. The person you talk to is the person who does the work. That is why we take a limited number of jobs at a time.
