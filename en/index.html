Questions

Questions we often get

How do you make sure the solution gets used?

We hand over the solution with a walkthrough in your own workflows, guidelines for safe use and follow-up during the pilot period. Otherwise use often ends up sporadic and dependent on individuals.

Which AI tool should we choose?

It depends on your systems and tasks. If you work in Microsoft 365, Copilot is often the natural choice. With few users, ChatGPT or Claude can be a cheaper and stronger starting point. We clarify this on the first call.

How quickly can we get started?

A defined task is typically live three to six weeks after the first call: one to two weeks of clarification and two to four weeks of setup and testing.

Can we put customer data into ChatGPT or Copilot?

Yes, when it happens in the business editions with a data processing agreement, where your data is not used to train the models and access follows your own permissions. Which data may be used where goes into your AI policy.

What happens if we stop working together?

You keep everything: setup, documentation and access. You only depend on us if you choose to.
