AI decision meeting for leadership

Half a day where owners and leadership decide what AI should be used for in your business and leave with a written plan.

What you decide in the meeting

  • Where time really goes in your business and where AI can take over some of it
  • Which tasks AI can handle for you today, and which should wait
  • Data, security and accountability: what leadership must decide
  • The first two tasks, with owner, goal and timeline
  • Budget: licences, setup and the option of an SMV:Digital grant

What you get afterwards

  • A written decision note with prioritised tasks
  • An estimate of price and time for the first setups
  • A tool recommendation for your systems

Who it is for

Owner-managers, executives and leadership teams in small and medium-sized businesses, typically 3 to 10 participants. The meeting takes place at your premises or online and is led by the consultant who also handles the setup afterwards if you decide to go ahead.

Questions

What happens after the meeting?

You get the decision note and decide for yourselves whether and when to go ahead. If you choose a setup, it is tested on your own tasks and handed over to the staff who will use it.

Do we need to prepare?

A short questionnaire before the meeting and ideally a list of the tasks that take the most time today.

Can the meeting be part of an SMV:Digital programme?

Yes, analysis and planning can be part of a grant programme if it is in your project description.
