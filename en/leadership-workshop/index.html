AI workshop for leadership

Half a day where owners and leadership decide what AI should be used for in your business and leave with a clear plan.

What we cover

  • Where time really goes in your business and where AI can take over some of it
  • Realistic expectations: what AI does well today and what it does not
  • Data, security and accountability: what leadership must decide
  • Choosing the first two tasks, with owner, goal and timeline
  • Budget: licences, setup and the option of an SMV:Digital grant

What you get afterwards

  • A written decision note with prioritised tasks
  • An estimate of price and time for the first setups
  • A tool recommendation for your systems

Who it is for

Owner-managers, executives and leadership teams in small and medium-sized businesses, typically 3 to 10 participants. At your premises or online. It is about direction and decisions for the business. The workshop is led by Ibrahim Sleiman, who also handles the setup afterwards if you choose to go ahead.

Workshops for staff

Once the direction is set, your staff can have workshops that build their knowledge of AI in their daily work. We develop them from your own tasks and the tools you already use, such as Microsoft 365, ChatGPT or Gemini, and are happy to run them together with someone from leadership.

  • Built on tasks from your own daily work, such as quotes, documents and customer enquiries
  • Rules for using AI and data, so staff know what they may do
  • Exercises in the tools you have licences for
  • Follow-up where we gather what people have learned and agree on next steps

Scope and price are agreed in a written offer.

Questions

Do you also run workshops for staff?

Yes. The leadership workshop is a working session where leadership makes decisions and gets a written plan with owner, goal and budget. Workshops for staff build knowledge and are based on the tasks the plan points to. They can be booked separately.

Do we need to prepare?

A short questionnaire before the workshop and ideally a list of the tasks that take the most time today.

Can the workshop be part of an SMV:Digital programme?

Yes, analysis and planning can be part of a grant programme if it is in your project description.
